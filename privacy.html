Privacy

These pages collect only what you type into the request-access form: your email address, and, if you choose to give them, your name and a line about what you would use it for.

Where it goes

When you press Request access, your request is sent to two places.

  • Our own server, at tantr.app: everything you typed, and which page you asked from (Shunya Systems or tantr). This is the copy I read.
  • Our analytics account at PostHog: your email address, which page you asked from, whether you gave a name and a reason (not what they say), the page that linked you here, and any campaign tags in the link.

Your browser also remembers your address on your own device, so the form can tell you that you have already asked.

What is not collected

No cookies are set, no tracking script is loaded, and nothing is recorded until you send the form.

What it is used for

To read your request and write back to you about access. Nothing else. It is not shared or sold.

Having it deleted

Write to me through LinkedIn and your request is deleted from both places.

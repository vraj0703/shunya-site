Privacy

These pages collect one thing: the email address you type into the waitlist form.

What is stored

When you press Join, your address is sent to our analytics account at PostHog, together with which page you joined from (Shunya Systems or tantr), the page that linked you here, and any campaign tags in the link. Your browser also remembers the address locally so the form can tell you that you are already on the list.

What is not

No cookies are set, no tracking script is loaded and nothing is recorded until you submit the form.

What it is used for

To write to you when the product you asked about opens. Nothing else, and it is not shared or sold.

Leaving the list

Write to us through LinkedIn and your address is deleted.

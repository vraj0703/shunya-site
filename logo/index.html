Shunya Systems · the mark

A zero, held by two braces: {0}

Shunya is the Sanskrit word for zero. The mark does not draw one. Two braces stand facing each other, and the space they leave between them is the zero.

The braces are the ones a programmer types. What they hold is empty, which is where every build starts.

On three grounds

Gold on ink is the mark at home. On paper and on gold it goes to ink.

Construction

One unit, u, and the golden ratio, φ = 1.618. Every fixed point of the mark sits on a power of φ times u. The outer sweep between those points is drawn by hand.
